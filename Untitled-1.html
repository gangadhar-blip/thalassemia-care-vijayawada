Thalassemia Treatment & Management

Blood Transfusion

Regular blood transfusions help maintain appropriate haemoglobin levels in patients with transfusion-dependent thalassemia.

Iron Chelation

Iron chelation therapy helps reduce excess iron accumulation caused by repeated transfusions.

Regular Monitoring

Regular blood tests and assessment of iron overload and organ health are important.

Curative Treatment

Hematopoietic stem-cell transplantation, commonly known as bone marrow transplantation, can offer a potential cure for selected patients with thalassemia.

Eligibility depends on several factors including the patient's age, health status and availability of a suitable donor.

Prevention of Thalassemia

Carrier Screening

Screening can identify individuals who carry thalassemia genes.

Genetic Counselling

Genetic counselling can help families understand inheritance and reproductive options.

Prenatal Diagnosis

Appropriate prenatal testing can help identify whether a pregnancy is affected.
